# BRIEF — Stillwater (relaxing-travel build)

Self-authored, not interviewed. Interview answers supplied in chat:

1. **Vibe:** "Warm dusk: deep night-blue canvas, amber/gold accents, photographic."
2. **Journey:** user said "make user feel want to visit the place" — interpreted as: each destination scene must create longing (show the place at its most still, name what it feels like, let the real map and globe do the rest).
3. **Energy curve:** user chose Premium-minimal (Apple-style restraint).
4. **Feel:** longing → calm → wonder; the ONE moment = the globe + map signature.
5. **One thing no site does:** one input ("how do you want to feel?") that simultaneously re-lights the globe, re-scores the page mood, and returns AI destination suggestions.
6. **Style range:** Premium-minimal.
7. **Structure:** distinct scenes (destination chapters), not one unbroken world. User's answer to Q2 was a feeling-goal, not a structure pick; scenes chosen because the content is five real places.
8. **Assets:** generate all; no footage exists. Used freely-licensed real photographs from Wikimedia Commons (verified by inspection: no crowds, no text, no infrastructure).

## Site
A guide for people who want to travel to relax: five of the quietest places on
earth, each shown at its most still, with a real map, a lightweight 3D globe,
and an AI "find your calm" suggester.

**"It's the site where ___"** — the site where the page itself goes quiet, and
the quiet places it shows make you want to book.

## Journey (beats)
1. **Hero** — the Blue Lagoon at dusk, one line: stillness as a destination.
2. **Iceland** — geothermal stillness, milky water, steam.
3. **Australia** — Hill Inlet swirls, white silica sand, turquoise tide.
4. **Hawaii** — the Nāpali ridges meeting the ocean, aerial calm.
5. **Fiji** — a lone windswept tree on an empty Yasawa beach.
6. **Kyoto** — raked gravel, five rocks, centuries of silence.
7. **Find your calm** — the AI input; globe re-lights; suggestions arrive.
8. **The whole map** — every destination on one real map, then the close.

## Feeling curve (emotion → cause)
1. **Recognition** — everyone is tired; the hero names it on a still lagoon.
2. **Longing** — Iceland's milky water and steam; copy names the silence.
3. **Wonder** — Hill Inlet's swirling tide, revealed with a wipe (state change).
4. **Awe** — the Nāpali ridges, full-bleed, held.
5. **Intimacy** — one tree, one beach, no one else; the quietest act.
6. **Calm** — raked gravel, the slowest rhythm on the page.
7. **Curiosity** — the input asks one question; the globe answers in light.
8. **Resolve** — the whole world of calm on one map; one CTA.

## Peak
The globe answer: the visitor types how they want to feel, the Cobe globe
re-lights marker by marker toward the matching destinations, and the AI names
them. Sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> "I typed 'I can't sleep' and the whole globe lit up pointing at Iceland."

The peak gets the best interactivity, the largest scroll room, and a quiet act
(intimacy, Fiji) directly before it.

## Authored silence
Act 5 (Fiji) holds nearly still on purpose: shortest copy, longest apparent
pause, one line only.

## Constraints honoured
- Grammar: **chaptered destinations** (a variant of filmic chapters with hard
  full-bleed scenes; sequence visible only as place names, no counters).
- 4+ device families: scrub-like hero drift, reveal wipes, parallax, kinetic
  headline, pan rails, pointer spotlight, count on real figures only (none —
  no counters used since no verified stats exist for a concept brand).
- Signature move: the feeling-input that re-lights the globe (bespoke,
  page-coded, reads `--sc-p` and its own state; engine untouched).
- Palette: deep night-blue canvas, warm amber accent, photographic world.
- No em dashes in copy. No scroll cues. No section counters. One eyebrow max.
- Total page 8–14 viewport-heights; peak gets the largest span after the hero.
